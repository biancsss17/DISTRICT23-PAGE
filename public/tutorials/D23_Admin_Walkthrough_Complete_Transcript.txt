D23 VISUAL EDITOR - PROCESS VIDEO GUIDE

Recorded on a disposable local database. No production data or accounts were used.

01. Sign in
Open /admin/login, enter your administrator email and password, then select Sign in.

02. Open the visual editor
Choose Content, Design, Media, Sections or Records. The page preview is on the right.

03. Change homepage text
Open Content > Home. Type the hero, button, statistics and section wording.

04. Change identity and links
Open Header & contact details, then Navigation and Footer to edit their text and links.

05. Change fonts and text style
In Design, choose a page part and set its font, size, weight, alignment and color.

06. Change background and spacing
Choose Original, Solid or Gradient; set colors and spacing, then check smaller previews.

07. Open the cover controls
In Media, select the photos and videos that should rotate on the homepage.

08. Upload a photo
Choose a supported image up to 10 MB. It appears in the library immediately.

09. Set logo and brand colors
Select the uploaded image for logo or favicon in Content > Brand, then choose colors.

10. Upload a video
Use the same Media upload control for a supported video up to 50 MB.

11. Photo size limit
Photos over 10 MB are rejected. Videos may be up to 50 MB.

12. Arrange cover slides
Use the arrows to reorder. Remove takes a slide out of rotation but keeps its file.

13. Edit homepage sections
Change each section heading, description, order and visibility.

14. Save a private page draft
Click Save draft to refresh the page preview without changing the public site.

15. Publish the page
Check the preview, then click Publish changes and verify the public homepage.

16. Undo page changes
Use Restore previous publication or Discard draft in Versions & administration.

17. Manage records
Open Records for News, Achievements or Officers. Record saves take effect immediately.

18. Create a News story
Enter title, slug, summary, content, status, date and optional media, then save.

19. Fix a form error
Read the message, correct the marked field, then save again.

20. Check media limits
Use at most 3 photos and 1 video per record, including the cover or portrait.

21. Verify the saved story
Reopen the record and use Preview to check its text and media.

22. Delete a record photo or video
Click the individual file button in the saved record and confirm deletion.

23. Edit, preview or delete News
Use Edit to update, Preview to inspect, or Delete to remove the whole story.

24. Manage an Achievement
Use the story form, add Achievement date, save a draft, then publish when ready.

25. Manage an Officer
Enter identity, position, term, biography and media; save and reopen the profile.

26. Delete a library video
In Media library, select Delete video on the exact file and confirm.

27. Log out
Check the public result, then open Versions & administration and click Log out.
